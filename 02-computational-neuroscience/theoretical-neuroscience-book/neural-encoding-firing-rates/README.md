# Ch1 — Neural encoding I: firing rates and spike statistics

How a spike train is described statistically — firing rate definitions
(spike-count vs. time-varying rate), the Poisson spike-train model, and
interspike-interval statistics (e.g. coefficient of variation as an
irregularity measure).

## Relevance

Foundational vocabulary for anything that treats spikes/EEG events as a
statistical signal rather than a raw waveform — relevant background before
[03-bci-signal-processing](../../../03-bci-signal-processing/)'s classifiers
and before Ch3 (decoding)/Ch4 (information theory) in this book.
